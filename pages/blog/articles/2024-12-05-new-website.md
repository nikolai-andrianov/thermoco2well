We are pleased to introduce the re-designed [THERMOCO2WELL](https://thermoco2well.dk) website, now featuring a new [tool](https://thermoco2well.dk/project) that allows users to simulate fluid flow in a wellbore during CO<sub>2</sub> injection. This feature is designed to support researchers and industry professionals working on carbon capture and storage (CCS) by providing a practical resource for analyzing CO<sub>2</sub> behavior in injection scenarios. The updated site offers a straightforward and data-driven approach to enhance your understanding of CCS processes.

The mathematical model behind the online simulation tool is a steady-state version of the model, described in [SPE-212235](https://onepetro.org/spersc/proceedings-abstract/23RSC/2-23RSC/D021S010R006/518396). For an slanted well with a prescribed CO<sub>2</sub> mass injection rate, pressure and temperature, the code calculates the pressure and temperature of injected CO2 inside of a tubing.
